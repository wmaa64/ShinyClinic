# ClinicPro 15-second vertical ad

- Finished video: `ClinicPro-15s-Vertical.mp4` (1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, 15 seconds)
- Voiceover transcript and timing: `voiceover.srt` and `voiceover.txt`
- Storyboard preview: `storyboard-preview.png`
- Editable scene renderer: `render.js`
- Video/audio builder: `make_video.py`

The video uses Egyptian Arabic on-screen copy, fast feature cuts, a gentle original music bed, and the requested **3-day free trial** call to action. Patient details in screenshot areas are obscured.

The MP4 currently has music but no spoken narration. The Egyptian Arabic speech request was rejected by the speech service with HTTP 403. The timed script is included so a recorded voice can be added in CapCut or another editor.
